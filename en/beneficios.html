Corporate wellbeing

Looking after your team is an investment with a measurable return.

Employees who play sport have more energy and perform better. But the effect doesn't stop there: it also changes how the whole company functions.

0%

Fewer sick days in companies that promote sport among their staff

0%

Less perceived stress among those who exercise regularly

Sources: 5th Adecco Absenteeism Report, Gallup State of the Global Workplace, Wellhub — Wellbeing Outlook, sector studies on physical activity and workplace health.

Five reasons

What changes in a company that moves.

01

Reduces sick leave

Teams that exercise regularly face day-to-day challenges in better physical and mental shape. The effect shows up in both physical and emotional sick leave.

02

Strengthens the employer brand

A company that looks after its staff's wellbeing becomes far more attractive, both to talent it wants to attract and to the people it already has, and it improves its overall corporate image.

03

Strengthens teamwork

Playing sport together — for example, in a tournament between departments — strengthens bonds and team spirit, and that shows up in collective performance.

04

Improves the work environment

Employees who exercise feel more balanced and less stressed, leading to more relaxed, positive and collaborative teams, and fewer internal conflicts.

05

Boosts self-esteem and soft skills

Physical activity improves confidence and builds skills like negotiation, conflict resolution and leadership.

I want this program for my company
·

We started because corporate wellbeing had become just a one-off yoga class.

Many companies try a yoga class, drop it after two months, and that's where "corporate wellbeing" ends. We believe something more complete is needed: regular physical activity, moments that genuinely bring the team together, and health knowledge people can apply outside the office.

That's why Boost Wellness doesn't subcontract: we train our own team of trainers, facilitators and speakers, and we design every program tailored to the company that requests it.
