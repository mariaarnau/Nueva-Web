How it works

From the first conversation to the first day of activity.

Here's how we launch your corporate sport program: the same team that designs it is the one that delivers it, start to finish.

01

We analyze what the team needs

A short conversation or a form: current activity level, goals — movement, cohesion, health — and schedule availability.

02

We design a tailor-made program

We combine physical activity, team building, events and talks based on what fits each company's culture and calendar.

03

Our own team delivers it

Boost Wellness trainers, facilitators and speakers, not loose freelancers or subcontracted third parties.

04

We get started right away

We adapt to the space available — office, gym or outdoors — and bring whatever equipment is needed ourselves.

05

We fine-tune month by month

We review participation and feedback every month to refine the program, with nothing to renegotiate.

Where we do it

No renovations, no new venue to rent.

01

The office

A large meeting room, a common area, or even the workspace itself cleared for ten minutes. We bring mats, bands and whatever else is needed.

02

A gym

When the company or the business park already has its own gym or fitness room, we use it as the venue for the activity.

03

Outdoor space

Parks, rooftops or terraces near the office. Perfect for activities, events or even outdoor talks.
